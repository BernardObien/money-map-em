Money Map · EM Extension v0.1 · prototype

Corridor rate transparency for emerging markets

An open extension of the MIT DCI Money Map for corridors, methods, and rails where operator-side ground truth changes the picture — mobile-money receive, EM-native remittance providers, stablecoin off-ramps, and P2P channels.

Built by Bernard O'bien · Cambio Last updated: — · Source on GitHub
Prototype note. The current DCI Money Map covers 10 US-originating corridors and shows Nigeria's best fee as 0.00% (data unavailability rendered as zero). This extension adds four corridors, ten+ methods per corridor including mobile-money and P2P rails, and explicit parallel-market FX benchmarking. Rates reflect published fee schedules and typical off-ramp spreads observed in live corridors — see the methodology note.
Sending
Purpose
An open, extensible prototype extending the DCI Money Map to corridors and methods where emerging-market retail users actually transact. Built to inform the September 9 DCI Global talk and as an input into potential collaboration with MIT DCI's Payments Dashboard project.
Methodology
Rates are August 2026 approximations from published provider schedules, off-ramp spreads, and Cambio corridor observations. FX benchmarks use parallel-market rates where a meaningful gap exists (NGN, ARS). Not live quotes — see roadmap for planned live-API integration.
Data: data.json — updated daily. Corrections, additional methods, or corridor requests: obien@mit.edu or open an issue on GitHub.
